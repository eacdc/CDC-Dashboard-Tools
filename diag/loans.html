Loan movement — opening, drawn, repaid, closing

Every borrowing account in both companies: secured and unsecured loans, OD and CC, packing credit, PCFC, bill discounting, term loans. Each period’s closing is the next one’s opening, so the statement can be read straight down and checked against Tally at any date in it. A loan outstanding is shown as a positive number — drawn adds to it, repaid takes it away · the party explainer · every client

WHICH BORROWINGS, OVER WHAT PERIOD

Which ledgers count is decided by the group Tally itself put them in — Secured Loans, Unsecured Loans, Bank OD/OCC, Loans (Liability) — plus a ledger under Current Liabilities whose name says packing credit, PCFC, cash credit, bill discounting, WCDL, term loan or buyer’s credit. Money we lent out (Loans & Advances) is never counted, and a ledger named like a loan but grouped elsewhere is listed as a near miss rather than silently dropped.
