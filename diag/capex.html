Capex — what was bought, when, for how much

Every posting that debits a fixed asset (or capital work in progress), as a dated list: the item, the amount, the voucher it is on, and who it was bought from. Each row also says how it was funded, because an addition is recorded the day the asset is capitalised and the money may have left earlier, later, or over five years — a press bought on term finance is not crores of cash gone in a day. Assets sold or scrapped are shown apart and never netted against what was bought · the party explainer · loan movement

WHAT PERIOD, WHICH COMPANY

Which ledgers count is decided by the group Tally itself put them in — anything under Fixed Assets, plus capital work in progress wherever it is filed. Depreciation and repairs are expenses, not capex: writing an asset down is not buying one. An accumulated-depreciation ledger filed under the asset group is excluded by name for the same reason.
