THE LEDGER, AS TALLY NAMES IT

In Tally: open the ledger, Alt+F2 for the period, F1 for Detailed so it lists vouchers rather than months, then Alt+E → Current → Excel. The dates above are filled in from the file’s own heading, so they cannot drift from what you exported.
