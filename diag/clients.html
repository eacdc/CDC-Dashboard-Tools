Every client, with what Tally knows about it

One row per customer — not per ledger: a customer carrying three spellings is one row, under the name the merges point at, exactly as every other page counts it. Its contact details are taken from whichever of those spellings actually carries them, because the current ledger is often the empty one. The sales figures and the first and last invoice dates come from the same fold the dashboard’s Sales Analysis tab reads, so a list handed to a salesperson cannot quietly disagree with the dashboard the same salesperson is looking at · the party explainer · every party at once
