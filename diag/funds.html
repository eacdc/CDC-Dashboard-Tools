Funds flow — where the money went, and how much of it was borrowed

Three balances Tally itself keeps — what customers owe us, what we owe suppliers, what we owe banks — plus what was spent on machines. The change in each over a period says where funds were absorbed and where they came from, and the two are set against each other: of everything that went into trade and machinery, this much arrived as new borrowing. Stock is not in it, deliberately — see the bottom of the page · loan movement · capex
